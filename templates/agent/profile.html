Support & Guidelines

What if the customer is unavailable?

Attempt calling the customer twice. If still unreachable, tap "Report an issue" from the order details screen and select "Customer unavailable". Never mark an order as delivered without handoff or customer OTP verification.

Pickup guidelines at store

Upon arriving at SuperMarket Store 01, confirm your arrival in the app. Verify item count with the store packing associate before confirming pickup and initiating the delivery route.
