Frequently Asked Questions

FreshMart follows an everyday low price (EDLP) direct sourcing model, purchasing staple grains, rice, oils, and FMCG products directly from agricultural mills and manufacturers in volume, passing maximum savings to households without middleman markups.
We offer 3 daily delivery slots (Morning: 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM, Afternoon: 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM, Evening: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM) plus 2-hour express delivery. Orders above ₹499 qualify for 100% free delivery.
We operate a hassle-free doorstep return policy. You can inspect all fresh fruits, vegetables, and groceries at the time of delivery and return anything unsatisfactory directly to our delivery agent for an instant refund.
We support Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa, Mastercard, RuPay debit/credit cards, and NetBanking through encrypted secure payment gateways.
